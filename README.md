# CineVault

Discover trending movies and TV shows, search titles by keyword, watch trailers, and find where to stream online with **CineVault**.

## What You Can Do on CineVault
- **Browse Movies & TV Shows:** Explore popular, trending, and catalog titles in a clean, mobile-friendly grid.
- **Instant Search:** Quickly find movies and series by title.
- **Watch Trailers:** Open movie details to watch trailers or jump straight to video results.
- **Check Where to Watch:** See streaming and watch options for each title in one place.

## Built for Speed & Mobile Browsing
CineVault is designed for fast loading and smooth browsing across phones, tablets, and desktop screens, making it easy to pick what to watch next without clutter.
